How the Simulator Works

Irradiance & Clouds

Cloud cover reduces GHI linearly: at 100% overcast, only ~20% diffuse irradiance reaches the panel. The slider applies a proportional scaling factor to the baseline P50 forecast.

Temperature Derating

Crystalline silicon PV modules lose ~0.4% efficiency per °C above the 25°C STC reference. A +15°C heat wave reduces output by approximately 6%. The formula: P_adj = P × (1 - 0.004 × max(0, T_offset)).

Soiling & Dust

Saharan dust storms can deposit thick layers reducing transmittance by 40-60%. The soiling slider applies a direct multiplicative loss: P_adj = P × (1 - soiling_fraction).
